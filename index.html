📖 Tournament Rules

Rules for all modes and refund policy
🪂 Battle Royale All · Solo · Duo · Squad
  1. Use only your registered Free Fire UID and IGN.
  2. Hacks, scripts, cheats, third-party tools, and emulators are not allowed.
  3. Join only in the team format selected for the match (Solo, Duo, or Squad).
  4. Room ID and password are shared by the admin when the match becomes live.
  5. Lobby issues or technical problems must be reported to the admin promptly.
  6. Rule violations may result in disqualification and forfeiture of rewards.
  7. Admin result verification and final decisions are binding.
Scoring (when enabled)☠️ Kill: +1 point🏆 Booyah: +10 points🥉 Top 3: +5 points🏅 Top 5: +3 points
⚔️ Clash Squad 2 vs 2 · 4 vs 4 (1 vs 1 disabled)
  1. Only registered UID and IGN may be used.
  2. No hacks, glitches, scripts, or unfair third-party tools.
  3. The room is created and managed by the admin.
  4. Players must follow the selected 2v2 or 4v4 team size and match settings.
  5. Abuse, teaming violations, or unfair play may lead to disqualification or a ban.
🐺 Lone Wolf 1 vs 1 · 2 vs 2
  1. Use your own registered UID and IGN.
  2. Hacks, scripts, glitches, and third-party unfair tools are prohibited.
  3. Room ID/password are provided by the admin when available.
  4. Follow the selected 1v1 or 2v2 mode and match settings.
  5. Any abuse or unfair play may result in disqualification.
🛡️ Refund Policy
  1. If the admin cancels a match, the full entry fee will be refunded.
  2. If a match cannot start because of a confirmed technical issue, the admin will review and process the applicable refund.
  3. Once a match has started, leaving voluntarily does not qualify for a refund.
  4. Refunds, when approved, are returned to the applicable in-app wallet.
  5. Cheating, abuse, or other rule violations are not eligible for a refund.
  6. For disputes, contact support and provide relevant proof such as screenshots or video.

Important
Check match details before joining. Match-specific settings and admin announcements take precedence where applicable.
